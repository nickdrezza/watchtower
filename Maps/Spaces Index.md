# Spaces Index

Three spaces, one rule each. Mixing them is hard rule 4. The `## Spaces` section in `AGENTS.md` gives the
rules.

| Space | For | Landing page |
|---|---|---|
| `Spaces/Work` | Your current job — projects, logs, meetings, reference | [[Spaces/Work/index|Work]] |
| `Spaces/Career` | Career planning, future roles, portable professional material | [[Spaces/Career/index|Career]] |
| `Spaces/Personal` | Personal notes, diary, memories, reflections, people, places | [[Spaces/Personal/index|Personal]] |

`Concepts/`, `Maps/`, `Dashboards/`, `_Templates/`, `_Docs/`, and `_Agents/` are **infrastructure**, not
a work area. Mixed or uncertain raw dumps go to `Inbox/Processed/`.
