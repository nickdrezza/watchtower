# Personal Vault Guide

The routing and writing contract for the personal space. Personal material is preserved in your own voice — an agent adds structure around it and never polishes it into an essay.

| Section | For |
|---|---|
| [[Diary]] | Dated daily entries |
| [[Memories]] | Past events, milestones, life history |
| [[Reflections]] | Opinions, values, identity questions, rambles |
| [[People]] · [[Locations]] · [[Pets]] | Personal entity notes |
| [[Uncategorized]] | Preserved material awaiting routing |

Records only what you actually said. Never infers a relationship, a date, or a place.
